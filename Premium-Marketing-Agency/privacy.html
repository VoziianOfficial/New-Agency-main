We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate this website, respond to enquiries and discuss requested services.

This policy applies to information handled through this website. It does not automatically apply to third-party websites, advertising platforms or services that may be linked from this site.

Overview

NOVA Performance provides performance marketing, Google Ads, lead generation, e-commerce advertising, analytics and automation services.

Visitors can browse the website without submitting a contact form. If you choose to contact us, the information you provide may be used to respond to your request and discuss relevant services.

Important

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card information, advertising-platform credentials or other confidential access information through the public contact forms on this website.

Information We May Collect

Information you provide

When you submit a website enquiry, the form may request information such as:

  • your name;
  • company or organisation name;
  • business email address;
  • website address;
  • business type;
  • approximate monthly advertising budget;
  • the service or area you need help with;
  • information included in your message or enquiry.

This website does not require a telephone number in its enquiry forms.

Technical information

Like many websites, the hosting environment or technical systems used to deliver the site may automatically process limited technical information.

  • IP address;
  • browser and device information;
  • requested pages;
  • date and time of requests;
  • basic server or security logs.

The exact information available depends on the hosting, security and technical services used for the website.

How We May Use Information

Information submitted through the website may be used for purposes including:

  • responding to enquiries and requests;
  • understanding the type of advertising or marketing support requested;
  • discussing a Google Ads account review or audit;
  • communicating regarding potential services;
  • operating, maintaining and improving the website;
  • protecting the website against spam, abuse and security threats;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations where required.

Information submitted for an enquiry should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate reason.

Cookies & Local Storage

This website may use essential browser storage or cookies where necessary for basic website functionality.

For example, the site stores a simple browser preference after the cookie notice has been accepted or dismissed so that the notice does not need to be shown repeatedly.

Item Purpose Type
Cookie notice preference Remembers whether the website notice has already been handled. Essential preference
Technical server data Supports website delivery, diagnostics and security. Technical

More information is available in the Cookie Policy .

Sharing of Information

Personal information should not be sold merely because it was submitted through a contact form.

Information may, however, be processed by service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website or handle communications.

Depending on the website setup, this may include providers involved in:

  • website hosting;
  • email delivery;
  • website security;
  • technical maintenance;
  • form processing or communication systems.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with a lawful requirement, protect legal rights or address suspected misuse of the website.

Data Retention

Information should generally be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including communication, administrative, security and legal requirements.

An enquiry may be retained while the related conversation or potential engagement remains active and for a reasonable period afterwards where appropriate.

Technical logs may be retained according to the configuration and retention practices of the relevant hosting or security provider.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information handled through the website.

No website, email system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should therefore avoid submitting sensitive credentials or unnecessary confidential information through public forms.

Account access

Google Ads, Analytics, Merchant Center, CRM or other account credentials should be exchanged only through an appropriate secure process, not through the public enquiry form.

Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the request, you may be able to ask about personal information associated with your website enquiry.

Requests may include asking us to:

  • confirm whether relevant information is held;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete information where appropriate;
  • stop certain communications;
  • answer questions regarding how submitted information is handled.

We may need sufficient information to verify that a request relates to the correct person or enquiry.

Third-Party Services

The website may refer or link to third-party platforms, websites or services.

Those third parties operate under their own terms, privacy notices and data practices. This Privacy Policy does not control how an external website or platform handles information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, technical systems or information-handling practices.

When the policy is updated, the date shown near the top of this page should also be updated.
